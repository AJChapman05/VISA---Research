Disqualifier: Remove the top-ranked company if its latest filing shows a material accounting issue, unresolved regulatory threat, or dependence on one customer for more than 25% of revenue.

## Business and revenue model

Visa is a global payments-technology company, not a lender: it runs VisaNet and facilitates authorization, clearing, and settlement among consumers, issuers, acquirers, and sellers. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 1, pp. 4–5]

It earns service revenue, data-processing revenue, international-transaction revenue, and other revenue, net of client incentives. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 7, p. 45]

The advantage is the scale and reach of the network: Visa reported nearly 14,500 financial-institution clients, nearly 5 billion payment credentials, acceptance at more than 175 million merchant locations, and 258 billion transactions processed by Visa in fiscal 2025. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 1, pp. 4–5, 7, 13–14]

## Performance and management’s prior explanation

Fiscal-2023 net revenue was $32.653 billion, up 11%; management attributed the increase primarily to nominal cross-border volume, processed transactions, and nominal payments volume, partly offset by higher client incentives. [Visa 2023 10-K, Item 7, p. 40]

Fiscal-2024 net revenue was $35.926 billion, up 10%; management again attributed the increase primarily to nominal cross-border volume, processed transactions, and nominal payments volume, partly offset by higher client incentives. [Visa 2024 10-K, Item 7, p. 44]

The operating story improved in 2025. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 7, pp. 40, 45] Net revenue rose to $40.0 billion from $35.9 billion in 2024 and $32.7 billion in 2023; management attributed the 2025 increase primarily to higher processed transactions, cross-border volume, and payments volume, partly offset by higher client incentives. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 7, pp. 40, 45]

The later result supports the prior management explanation directionally: management cited the same core volume drivers in fiscal 2023, 2024, and 2025, while reported net revenue rose in each year. [Visa 2023 10-K, Item 7, p. 40] [Visa 2024 10-K, Item 7, p. 44] [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 7, pp. 40, 45]

Data-processing revenue rose 13% and international-transaction revenue 12% in 2025, while value-added-services revenue increased to $10.9 billion from $8.8 billion in 2024 and $7.2 billion in 2023. Net income rose to $20.1 billion from $19.7 billion in 2024 and $17.3 billion in 2023. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 7, pp. 40, 45] [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Note 3, p. 72]

What worsened is equally important. GAAP operating expenses grew 30% in 2025, versus 11% revenue growth, principally because the litigation provision rose to $2.6 billion from $0.5 billion and personnel expense increased. Client incentives rose to $15.8 billion from $13.8 billion, reducing the conversion of gross revenue into net revenue. Operating income therefore increased only to $24.0 billion from $23.6 billion, despite the larger revenue base. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 7, pp. 40, 45–46] [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Operations, pp. 59–60]

Free cash flow, calculated from cited 10-K inputs, was $21,577 million in fiscal 2025 ($23,059 million operating cash flow − $1,482 million purchases of property, equipment and technology). [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 64]

Free-cash-flow margin, calculated from cited 10-K inputs, was 53.94% in fiscal 2025 ($21,577 million ÷ $40,000 million). [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 64] [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p. 59]

For comparison, free cash flow, calculated from cited 10-K inputs, was $18,693 million and free-cash-flow margin, calculated from cited 10-K inputs, was 52.03% in 2024, versus $19,696 million and 60.32% in 2023. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 64] [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p. 59] Thus, 2025 cash generation improved from 2024 in dollars and margin, but the margin remained below 2023. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows, p. 64] [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 8, Consolidated Statements of Operations, p. 59]

## Strategy, risks, and challenges

Management’s fiscal-2025 strategy was to deepen card-based consumer payments and expand into non-card payments, including tap, tokens, cross-border capabilities, consumer credit, commercial and money-movement solutions, and value-added services; it also cited investment in GenAI, stablecoins, and agentic commerce. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 1, pp. 4–5, 7, 13–14]

The July 28, 2026 release reported fiscal Q3 2026 net revenue of $11.6 billion, up 14% year over year, driven by growth in payments volume, cross-border volume, and processed transactions. [Visa Reports Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Results, July 28, 2026, paragraph under “Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 — Financial Highlights”]

The risks are that regulation, alternative payment rails, price competition, and client incentives can erode economics; Visa specifically identifies competition from real-time-payment networks and other lower-priced facilitators. A material network disruption or cyber incident could also damage transaction availability, trust, and revenue. Management’s growth ambitions carry execution, compliance, privacy, and cybersecurity risk as the company expands into new technologies and payment forms. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 21–28, 31–33]

## Unknowns

- **Ultimate litigation outcome and cash effect:** Visa states that it cannot estimate a potential loss or range of loss at trial if negotiated resolutions are not reached; the ultimate amount and timing therefore remain uncertain. [Visa 2025 10-K, Note 20—Legal Matters, p. 100]
- **Effect of alternative payments and real-time-payment competition on growth, pricing, and incentives:** Unknown. Visa identifies competing alternative-payment systems, faster-payment initiatives, and pricing and incentive pressure as risks that could harm net revenue and profits. [Visa 2025 10-K, Item 1A, pp. 27–28]
- **Specific future M&A target or plan:** Not disclosed in the reviewed materials. This does not prove that Visa will not make future acquisitions.
